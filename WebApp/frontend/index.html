About the club

A classroom that answers to a P&L.

WSIC exists to teach fundamental investing by doing it with money that can actually be lost. We meet weekly in the Goddard School of Business, pitch and defend positions in front of the group, and vote on every trade before it goes in.

How a pitch becomes a position

A sector team researches a company for two to three weeks, builds a valuation model, and presents a thesis to the full club. Members question the pitch directly — on the numbers, not the delivery. A trade needs a majority vote and sign-off from the portfolio committee before it's placed.

Who can join

Any enrolled Weber State student, any major. Most members are in finance or economics, but some of our best sector leads have come from engineering and the sciences. New members start on a sector team; you don't need prior trading experience.

Support the club

Two ways to back what we're building.

Every dollar we manage or spend is visible to the person who gave it. Gifts route through the Weber State University Foundation and are tax-deductible where applicable.

  • Fund capital

    Added directly to the pool the club invests. It shows up as a position on the portfolio page like any other dollar we manage, and it compounds — or doesn't — right alongside the rest of the fund.

  • Operating support

    Covers what trading gains don't: travel to case competitions, CFA exam vouchers for graduating members, Bloomberg Terminal access, and guest speaker honoraria.
